Peptic Ulcer Disease; Stomach and Gastric Ulcers – A Concise Review

A clinical overview of peptic ulcer disease summarizing etiology, diagnostics, and evidence-based management strategies for gastroenterology teams.

2023 · International Research Journal of Gastroenterology and Hepatology 6 (1), 30–39 Co-authors: Amina Chaudhry · Kimberly Morton Cuthrell · Owen R. Thornton

Scope

The review distills current literature on the pathogenesis of peptic ulcer disease, including Helicobacter pylori infection, NSAID exposure, and stress-related mucosal damage. It outlines diagnostic pathways and therapeutic interventions ranging from proton pump inhibitors to surgical escalation.

Key Highlights

  • Contrasts duodenal and gastric ulcer presentations with attention to alarm symptoms and differential diagnosis.
  • Summarizes eradication regimens for H. pylori alongside antimicrobial resistance considerations.
  • Details lifestyle and nutritional modifications that support pharmacologic therapy.
  • Discusses complications such as perforation and bleeding, emphasizing rapid triage and multidisciplinary care.

Clinical Relevance

By synthesizing gastroenterology, pharmacology, and patient education insights, the article equips clinicians and trainees with a concise decision-support resource for managing acute and chronic ulcer cases.
