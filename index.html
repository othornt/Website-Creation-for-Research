About

Owen R. Thornton is a neuroscience undergraduate researcher whose work spans endurance performance psychology, affective neuroscience, and mental health epidemiology. He studies how neuroplasticity, decision-making, and emotional regulation intersect with clinical conditions such as borderline personality disorder and depression while bringing the perspective of an elite ultramarathon athlete to questions of human resilience.

Research Interests

  • Borderline personality disorder and neurocomputational modeling
  • Neuroplasticity, neuroendocrine modulation, and mood disorders
  • Psychological drivers of ultramarathon performance
  • Global mental health surveillance and equity

Current Focus

Synthesising cross-disciplinary literature to illuminate biomarkers and behavioral strategies that translate from laboratory settings to high-stakes endurance contexts and emerging clinical interventions.
